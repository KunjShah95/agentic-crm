# Prospecting, outreach and discovery

**Date:** 2026-10-03 · Companion to [`docs/gtm/offer.md`](offer.md)

Volume is not the constraint. Twenty researched conversations produce one
pilot; two hundred blasted ones produce a blocked number and a DPDP problem.
Everything below is built for 20–30 contacts, hand-written, sent by a human.

---

## Who to target

Ahmedabad residential builders and brokerages, 5–50 people, currently running
on a spreadsheet plus a WhatsApp Business account.

### Disqualify early

- Chains, franchises, or anything with a central IT team. Long cycle, and
  they will want features you will build for them for free.
- Anyone already using a CRM daily. Genuinely good CRM users do not buy this.
- Under ~20 active enquiries a month. The economics do not work and they will
  churn.
- Anyone who asks for it to send bulk WhatsApp on their behalf. That is the
  exact use case you just hardened against, and you would be selling them a
  way to get their number banned.

### Qualify with

- Website or Instagram that looks maintained.
- A project micro-site or a portal presence (99acres, MagicBricks).
- 3BHK+ residential inventory, priced in crore — the segment where a
  cost sheet and a CLP actually matter.
- A named owner or sales head you can reach, not a generic inbox.

## What to record per prospect

One row. Ten minutes of research each. This is what makes the outreach a
conversation instead of a template.

```
Business / contact / role
Projects + unit count + price band          (does inventory matter to them?)
Where enquiries actually arrive             (Instagram? 99acres? Walk-in?)
What they answer today                      (spreadsheet? paper? nothing?)
Whether they respond in evenings            (the ₹-unmeasurable leak)
Site visits happening but not recorded?     (the strongest single question)
Do they issue RERA documents manually?      (PDF from a consultant, Word, WhatsApp?)
Do they track broker/channel commission?    (spreadsheet again?)
Do they send demand letters manually?       (CLP relevance)
Instagram posts in the last 30 days?        (are they actually marketing?)
The ONE sentence you would say to them
```

That last field is the whole point. If you cannot write it, you have not
researched them; do not send.

## Outreach

Send one channel, from a person, with an opt-out. Never a bulk tool.

**WhatsApp (primary — they live here)**

> Namaste [Name] ji — [Your name] from Estate360, we build the sales system for
> builders in Ahmedabad. I noticed [specific thing: their 4BHK project in
> Bopal / their Instagram post about possession].
>
> One question: when an enquiry comes in at 9pm and your salesperson doesn't
> reply, does anyone pick it up the next morning?
>
> We built a system that scores and assigns it automatically, keeps the site
> visit and the cost sheet attached, and generates the RERA document from the
> booking. Happy to show it on your own project data — 15 minutes, no charge.
>
> If this isn't useful, tell me and I won't message again.

Why it works: the question is diagnostic rather than promotional, it implies a
failure they have felt, the ask is small and concrete, and there is a real
opt-out.

**Follow-up, once, four days later** — and only once:

> [Name] ji — following up once. The short version: your leads, site visits,
> inventory and booking documents in one place, with the paperwork that
> usually gets lost. If it's not a priority right now, no problem — should I
> check back in three months or not at all?

Then stop. A second follow-up on an unanswered WhatsApp is where accounts get
reported.

**Email** — only to a real address found on their site, same content, shorter.
**Call** — for the five best fits, to reach the owner directly. A call that
gets "send me details" means they were not the right contact; go back to the
list.

## Discovery call

Twenty minutes. Twelve questions. The first three matter most.

1. Walk me through the last enquiry you had, start to finish. What arrived,
   who saw it, what happened?
2. What time did it arrive, and what time did the buyer first hear back?
3. If that salesperson was unavailable, what happened to it?
4. Where do most enquiries come from? Which of those do you actually pay for?
5. What do you use today to keep track — and when did you last open it?
6. When a buyer says "I'll decide next week", what happens?
7. Who follows up after a site visit, and what do they write down?
8. How do you decide your discount? Does everyone see the same numbers?
9. Who generates your booking document, and how long does it take?
10. Do you work with channel partners? How do you track what they're owed?
11. What happens to a buyer if the possession date slips? Who tells them?
12. If I fixed one thing this month, which one?

**Listen for:** "we just check it sometimes", "actually my assistant keeps a
list", "the WhatsApp goes to everyone", "we tried [CRM] but nobody used it",
"the builder does that part".

That last family — where the owner has delegated the actual process to one
dedicated person — is your pilot. That person is your champion, they are
already doing the work manually, and they have been promised nothing. Give
them the tool and the title and the pilot is close.

**Close with a next action, not a proposal:**

> Then here's what I'd do. Two weeks, one project, ₹25,000. Week one I only
> look — I count your last 90 days of enquiries and we agree on one number to
> hit. If we miss it, you don't pay for month two. Can I send two pages?

## The pilot conversation, when they say yes

1. **Name the champion.** Who is the person who will actually do this?
2. **Get the spreadsheet.** Inventory + last 90 days of enquiries. No
   exceptions — you cannot baseline retroactively later.
3. **Agree the number.** One, written, on paper.
4. **Collect what you need:** WhatsApp Business number, Meta page (if ads),
   portal logins for exports, RERA numbers per project.
5. **Send the invoice and the credential pack.** The ingest secret is issued
   from their workspace settings — do not put it in a shared doc or a
   screenshot.
6. **Set a day-3 check-in.** Not a launch party. A 15-minute call to see what
   broke.

## Tracking

One table. Update it the same day, or it becomes fiction.

| Prospect | Source | Sent | Replies | Calls | Pilot | Setup fee | Blocker |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Review weekly: replies → calls → pilots. If reply rate is under ~15% after 30
sent, the message is wrong, not the list — change the question, not the
segment. If calls are high and pilots are zero, the offer or the price is
wrong. If pilots are zero but calls say "interested", you are losing in the
close; go reread `offer.md` § Objections.
